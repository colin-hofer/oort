## Design Context

### Users

The support-operations example is for support managers and team leads checking queue health between meetings. They need to spot SLA breaches, overloaded teams, and the oldest actionable tickets quickly. This context applies to `examples/support-operations`; the Oort control plane keeps its existing visual system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, operational, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a well-kept dispatch board: serious about urgency without manufacturing anxiety.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light industrial control-room aesthetic with warm paper neutrals, ink typography, rust accents, crisp rules, and dense but readable operational data. Avoid generic card grids, glossy SaaS gradients, glass effects, and decorative charts.

### Design Principles

- Put SLA risk and ownership ahead of decorative metrics.
- Use restrained color semantically; urgency must never depend on color alone.
- Keep the dashboard scannable at desktop and fully usable on narrow screens.
- Prefer native browser controls and bundled assets so the deployed app remains fast and CSP-safe.
- Show where the data came from and when the snapshot was current.
